OFFICIAL COLLEGE NOTICES AND GUIDELINES ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027

1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS AND CONDONATION POLICY
- A minimum aggregate attendance of 75% is mandatory for all undergraduate and postgraduate students to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for medical condonation by submitting valid hospital medical certificates and parental approval within 5 working days of absence.
- Students having less than 65% aggregate attendance will be strictly detained and must repeat the semester courses.

2. SEMESTER EXAMINATION SCHEDULE AND RULES
- The mid-semester examinations will commence from 10 November 2026.
- The final end-semester theory examinations are scheduled to start from 15 December 2026.
- Students must carry their official College ID Card and printed Hall Ticket to all examination halls.
- Electronic gadgets, smart watches, mobile phones, and programmable calculators are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.
- Minimum passing criteria is 40% in internal continuous assessment and 40% in end-semester theory exam, with an overall aggregate of 45%.

3. CAMPUS PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY AND REGISTRATION
- Final-year B.Tech, M.Tech, and MCA students are eligible for Campus Recruitment Drives.
- The minimum academic eligibility criterion is 60% or 6.5 CGPA throughout 10th, 12th, and all completed college semesters, with no active backlogs.
- Eligible students must register on the Training and Placement Cell Portal before 30 October 2026.
- Required documents to keep ready: Updated resume (PDF), two passport photos, college identity card, and all semester marks sheets.
- Dress code for all technical and HR interviews is strictly formal business attire.

4. COLLEGE AND LIBRARY WORKING HOURS
- Regular college lecture hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.
- The Central Library remains open from 8:30 AM to 7:00 PM on working days, and 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays.
- Digital Library access with IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect journals is available 24/7 via the campus LAN and student portal credentials.

5. FEE PAYMENT AND SCHOLARSHIPS
- Semester tuition fee payment without penalty is due on or before 25 November 2026.
- Government and merit scholarship applications must be submitted to the Academic Dean's Office by 05 November 2026.
